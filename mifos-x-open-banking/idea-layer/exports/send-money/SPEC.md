## Overview

Send Money is the Pay-tab **hub**: a recipient/method launcher, not an amount form. It has three sections — **Recent Recipients** (a horizontal row of circular avatars: a "New" entry plus the most-recently-paid beneficiaries, ordered by their latest transaction), **Choose a way to send** (the working rails only — "Local transfer · Free · Arrives instantly"; international and mobile-money rails are intentionally omitted because OBP has no reliable backing for them here), and **All Beneficiaries** (every counterparty flagged `is_beneficiary`, shown as avatar + name + `"{scheme} · {bankName} ·· {last4}"`).

Tapping a recipient or beneficiary opens the amount-entry step with that recipient preselected; "New" / "Local transfer" opens amount entry with the beneficiary picker. Amount entry → Confirm Payment (`send-money-confirm`), which executes the transfer.

Payments are sent via the OBP **COUNTERPARTY** transaction-request (`POST .../owner/transaction-request-types/COUNTERPARTY/transaction-requests`) addressing the payee by `counterparty_id` — this works for every beneficiary regardless of routing scheme (SEPA-by-IBAN only works for IBAN counterparties, and most sandbox payees are OBP-scheme). Bank display names resolve via the Banks API; recency comes from the account's owner transactions.
