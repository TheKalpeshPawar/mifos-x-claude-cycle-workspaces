## Overview

The Direct Debits screen displays all active merchant-initiated payment mandates for the consumer. Unlike standing orders (user-initiated recurring payments), direct debits are authorised by the account holder but executed by the payee on agreed collection dates. The screen lists current mandates with payee name, collection amount, frequency, next date, and status badge. Users can view mandate details and set up new direct debits via a floating action button.
