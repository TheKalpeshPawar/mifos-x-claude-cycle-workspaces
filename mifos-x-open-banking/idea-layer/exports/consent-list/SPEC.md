## Purpose

Loads locally-stored ConsentId(s) and fetches current status from `hsbc-obie-ais-v4.0:account-access-consents/{ConsentId}` via Ktorfit. Partitions results into active (Authorised) and history (Expired/Revoked) sections rendered in separate list segments with a divider. Computes `days_until_expiry` from `Data.ExpirationDateTime` — when ≤14 days a reconfirm urgency chip renders on the card and a warning banner appears at the top of the screen (OBIE 90-day PSU reconfirmation requirement, FR-008). HTTP 401 transitions to the dedicated `error_auth` state with a 'Sign in again' CTA rather than the generic retry flow. Card tap navigates to consent-detail with ConsentId as route parameter.
