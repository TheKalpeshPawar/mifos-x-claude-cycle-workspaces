## Overview

The Meetings screen gives field officers a weekly calendar strip and a chronological list of their upcoming meetings. A 7-day strip header lets officers jump to any day of the week; the currently selected day is highlighted in brand purple. Meeting cards display time, type chip (Online/In-Person/Phone), customer name, duration, and location, with a colored left accent border differentiating meeting types. Officers can join online meetings directly, add notes, and schedule new meetings via a bottom sheet form. The screen is navigated to from the fo-dashboard stat card and meeting rows.
