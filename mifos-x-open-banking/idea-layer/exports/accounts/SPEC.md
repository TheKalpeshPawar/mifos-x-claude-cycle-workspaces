# Feature Specification — My Accounts
**Feature:** accounts | **Flavor:** consumer | **Status:** enriched | **Quality Score:** 80

---

## Overview

The My Accounts screen is the primary banking hub for the Mifos X Open Banking consumer app, displaying all accounts held by the authenticated user under a single bank. Users can filter accounts by type (All, Checking, Savings, Business), see live balances with currency-coded color accents, and drill into any account for full details. A totals footer aggregates balance across all visible accounts, and a FAB provides quick access to request a new account.

## States

|---|---|---|
