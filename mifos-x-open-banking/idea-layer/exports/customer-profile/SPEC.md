## Overview

The Customer Profile screen renders the complete structured record for a single customer, surfacing personal identity, address, and employment information in clearly sectioned rows. Field officers can view all data in a read-optimized label/value layout, tap phone/email fields to initiate contact, and switch into edit mode to update information. The screen is the destination of "View Full Profile →" from the Customer 360 tab view and serves as the authoritative record view before and after KYC.
