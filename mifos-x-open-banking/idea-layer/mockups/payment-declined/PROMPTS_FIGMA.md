## State: content (PSU declined / access_denied)

**Prompt:** Design a full-screen mobile terminal screen (390px wide, no top bar, no bottom nav) for a PISP payment-declined outcome. Background #F9FAEF. Vertical column layout, centre-aligned, 24dp padding.

**Icon:** Material Icons "cancel" glyph, 88dp, colour #BA1A1A, centred. No animation — calm rendering.

**Title:** "Payment not completed" — Outfit/headline_medium (28sp), #1A1C16, bold 700, centre, 4dp below icon.

**Assurance line** (directly below title, 4dp gap): "Good news: no money has left your account." — Outfit/title_small (14sp/500), colour #386663, semibold 600, centre. This line must appear BEFORE the explanation body.

**Body:** "You didn't finish authorising this payment at HSBC, so it wasn't sent. You can review the details below and try again." — Outfit/body_medium (14sp), #44483D, centre, 24dp below assurance.

**Details card** (full content width): White (#FFFFFF) filled card, 16dp corner radius, 1dp border colour #FFDAD6, 16dp padding. Inside: "£250.00" in display-small (Outfit 36sp, bold 700, #1A1C16, centre) — use neutral black, NOT error red. Below: "To Jordan Avery — Barclays Bank UK" in body-large (Outfit 16sp, #44483D, centre). Below that, a horizontal row with space-between: label "Reason" (Outfit/body_medium, #44483D) on the left; value "Authorisation cancelled at HSBC" (Outfit/body_medium, #1A1C16, bold 500) right-aligned.

**Reasons card** (full content width, 16dp below details card): White (#FFFFFF) filled card, 16dp corner radius, 16dp padding. Heading "This can happen if:" (Outfit/title_small, #1A1C16, bold 700). Three body-medium (#44483D) text items below: (1) "You chose to cancel on the HSBC approval screen" (2) "The approval timed out before it was confirmed" (3) "HSBC couldn't confirm the payment from your account". Each item spaced with 4dp bottom margin.

**Primary CTA:** Full-width filled button below the reasons card: "Try again", #4C662B fill, #FFFFFF text (Outfit/label_large 14sp/500), 12dp corner radius, 16dp padding.

**Secondary CTA:** Full-width text button below primary: "Back to home", #386663 text (Outfit/label_large 14sp/500), 16dp padding.

**No** `declined_status_note` in this state.
