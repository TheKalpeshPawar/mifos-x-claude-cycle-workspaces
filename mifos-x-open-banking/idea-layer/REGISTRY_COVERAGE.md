## Per-Registry Coverage

### Screens — 23

Canonical roster is `idea-layer/_list.json`, not a `screens/` glob. 20 ship in source; 3
(`send-money`, `payment-consent`, `payment-status`) are P3 specifications with no feature module.

Two screens have no module of their own: `licences` lives in `feature/settings`,
`user-onboarding` in `feature/login`.

### Analytics tags — 82

Every one of the 23 screens has a `screen_view_*` tag. Generated 2026-07-28 by the reverse sync;
**not regenerated since the PISP features were added**, so the three P3 screens' interaction tags
are inherited from the screen-view sweep rather than authored per-action.

### DTOs — 24

AIS coverage is complete for every consumed resource. PIS registers 5 (the domestic-single subset
this project scopes) against **130 files shipping in source** across all six OBIE payment types —
the difference is scope, not omission.

### Flows — 9, and this is the one real gap

`flow_ref` is declared on only **9 of 23** `docs.yaml`. Per-flow cache views therefore cover
**3 of 9 flows**. The 14 screens without a `flow_ref` are reachable and correct, but they do not
appear in any flow-scoped view, so a flow-level audit under-reports them.

Owner: `/idea-flows`. Carried over from the 2026-07-30 handover, unchanged.

### i18n — 545 declared / 517 referenced

**0 missing** — verified with a real YAML parse over the full nested key tree, not a grep. The
28 declared-unreferenced keys are not orphans: they are referenced from `flow.yaml`, `docs.yaml`
and `app-shell.yaml`, which this count does not scan.

Two keys were removed on 2026-07-30 (`home.empty.cta` + `.accessibility`) in the **same edit** as
the component they labelled — deliberately, because the earlier `quick_actions_card` removal
orphaned 9 keys by not sweeping.

### Test scenarios — a known gap

`account-detail` has **14 test scenarios with empty descriptions** — ids exist, assertions
undocumented. The only feature in the corpus in that state. `direct-debits` and `standing-orders`
have no coverage for their `Unsupported` state, their most distinctive behaviour.

Owner: `/idea-test-generate`.

### Accessibility labels — 2 gaps

`settings/app_version_row` and `transactions/tx_category_tag` lack `accessibility_label`. Both are
recorded in their feature mockups.
