1. Introduction and Agreement

Mifos-X Open Banking ("the App") is a Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) open-source banking application that delivers two personas — Consumer (retail banking self-service) and Field Officer (agent banking) — from a single codebase on Android, iOS, desktop, and web platforms.

By installing, accessing, or using the App, you ("User") agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions ("Terms"). If you do not agree, do not install or use the App.

Separate banking terms apply: These Terms govern your use of the App software only. Separate terms of service from your banking institution govern the financial products and banking services you access through the App.

2. Description of the Application

2.1 Solution — "Banking for Everyone"

Mifos-X Open Banking is a dual-persona KMP banking super-app powered by the Open Bank Project (OBP) API v7.0.0. The App connects retail customers and banking field officers to financial services via a single, secure interface.

PersonaFeatures
Consumer
Retail Banking		 Account overview & balances · Transaction history · Send Money & payment initiation · Beneficiary management · Debit/credit card management · Standing orders · ATM locator · FX rates
Field Officer
Agent Banking		 Customer search & detail · New customer onboarding (KYC) · Corporate account onboarding · KYC document review & compliance · Account application management · Customer messaging · Meeting management

The App connects exclusively to the OBP REST API endpoint configured by the deploying banking institution. It is a client application only — it does not own or operate a database, backend, or financial infrastructure.

3. User Eligibility and Accounts

You must be at least 18 years old and legally capable of entering binding agreements. Field Officers must hold valid authorization credentials issued by their employing banking institution.

You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential, logging out on shared devices, and notifying your banking institution immediately of any suspected unauthorized access.

4. Permitted and Prohibited Uses

4.1 Permitted

  • Access your own banking accounts and financial data via the connected OBP API
  • Initiate payments and transactions for which you are authorized
  • Perform Field Officer duties (if licensed) including customer onboarding and KYC
  • Export your own financial data for personal record-keeping

4.2 Prohibited

  • Access accounts or data of other individuals without explicit authorization
  • Attempt to reverse-engineer or extract authentication credentials from the App
  • Perform automated scraping or API abuse beyond normal single-user operation
  • Use the Field Officer persona without valid institutional authorization
  • Conduct activities violating AML, KYC, or sanctions regulations
  • Use the App for fraudulent, deceptive, or money-laundering activities

Violations may result in termination of access and referral to law enforcement.
