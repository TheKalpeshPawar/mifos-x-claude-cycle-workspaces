How UK Open Banking works

  1. how_to_reg
    1. You approve the permission request We ask HSBC to prepare a consent with the list of data categories. You are redirected to HSBC's secure portal to review and approve it — choosing which accounts to include.
  2. login
    2. You authenticate directly with HSBC You log in on HSBC's portal using your HSBC credentials and complete Strong Customer Authentication. Mifos Open Banking never sees your password at any point.
  3. shield
    3. A secure token is returned to us HSBC sends a short-lived access token back using FAPI 1.0 Advanced — a banking-grade security protocol. We use it to read the data you approved; it cannot move money.

You can revoke this access at any time from Settings → Manage consents, or directly from your HSBC app or HSBC online banking.
