What we never do

  • money_off
    No payments or transfers This app is Account Information only (AISP). We have no permission to initiate payments or move money from your account.
  • lock
    We never see your password You authenticate directly on HSBC's secure portal using Strong Customer Authentication (SCA). Your credentials never leave HSBC.
  • cancel
    Revoke access anytime You can disconnect HSBC data sharing at any time from Settings → Manage consents, or directly from your HSBC app.
