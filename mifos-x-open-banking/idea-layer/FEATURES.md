## Feature Dependencies

```
consent-onboarding
   └── consent-dashboard
          └── settings
                 └── licences

accounts ──┬── balances
           ├── transactions
           ├── beneficiaries
           ├── standing-orders
           ├── direct-debits
           ├── scheduled-payments
           ├── statements
           ├── product
           └── account-holder

home ── depends on ──> accounts · balances · transactions

send-money ── depends on ──> accounts · beneficiaries · payment-consent · payment-status
   ├── payment-consent  (depends back on send-money)
   └── payment-status   (depends back on send-money)
```

**`accounts` is the hub.** Nine features depend on it, because every account-scoped resource is
keyed by `accountId` and reached from account detail's Explore list.

**The PISP trio is mutually dependent by design.** `send-money` owns the idempotency key and the
staged `Initiation`; its two children hand results *back* as events rather than navigating, so the
parent retains ownership. That is why the graph has a cycle where the AIS features have none.
